2025-11-30
Downloaded Online Retail II dataset from UCI Machine Learning Repository.
Saved main transactions sheet as UTF-8 CSV in folder data_raw.

2025-12-01
Created PostgreSQL database online_retail.
Ran sql/01_create_tables.sql to create tables: raw_orders, clean_orders, returns_orders, order_level, customer_metrics, product_metrics.
Imported online_retail_II_utf8.csv into raw_orders through pgAdmin.

2025-12-02
Ran initial profiling queries on raw_orders to review ranges for quantity, unit_price, invoice_date_raw, customer_id, and country.
Confirmed presence of negative quantities for returns and some non-numeric values in numeric columns.

2025-12-03
Ran sql/02_cleaning.sql to populate clean_orders and returns_orders.
Steps in script:
– Cast quantity, unit_price, customer_id to numeric types.
– Parsed invoice_date_raw into invoice_ts (timestamp) and invoice_date (date).
– Derived invoice_month and invoice_quarter.
– Computed line_revenue as quantity * unit_price.
Verified:
– clean_orders holds only positive quantity rows.
– returns_orders holds only negative quantity rows.

2025-12-03
Built aggregated tables through sql/02_cleaning.sql:
– order_level: one row per invoice with order_revenue, customer_id, country, invoice_date.
– customer_metrics: one row per customer with total_revenue, order_count, first_order_date, last_order_date.
– product_metrics: one row per product with units_sold, total_revenue, avg_line_revenue.

2025-12-04
Created sql/03_analysis_queries.sql to group analysis by question Q1–Q8.

Q1 – Revenue by period
Ran queries for monthly and quarterly revenue based on clean_orders.
Exported results to:
– data_clean/monthly_revenue.csv
– data_clean/quarterly_revenue.csv

Q2 – Customer value and deciles
Ran queries on customer_metrics to:
– rank customers by total_revenue
– compute revenue share by decile
Used results for customer concentration metrics in findings.

Q3 – Product performance
Ran queries on product_metrics to rank products by total_revenue and units_sold.
Exported top revenue products to:
– data_clean/top_products_revenue.csv

Q4 – Country performance
Ran queries on order_level to compute order_count, total_revenue, and avg_order_value by country.
Exported results to:
– data_clean/countries_revenue.csv

Q5 – Order value and bands
Ran queries on order_level to compute global average order_revenue, median, and range.
Defined three order value bands: low (< 50), mid (50 to < 150), high (≥ 150).
Summarised order_count and total_revenue by band.

Q6 – New vs repeat customers
Joined order_level with customer_metrics to label each order as first or repeat based on first_order_date.
Computed revenue share, order_count, and avg_order_value for first vs repeat orders.
Exported summary to:
– data_clean/new_vs_repeat_orders.csv

Q7 – Returns and net revenue
Computed:
– gross_revenue from clean_orders
– returns_value from returns_orders
– net_revenue and returns_rate (returns_value / gross_revenue)
Joined product_metrics with returns_orders to find products with highest returns_value and returns_rate.
Exported product level results to:
– data_clean/returns_by_products.csv
Ran similar query by country and exported to:
– data_clean/returns_by_country.csv

Q8 – Customer spend tiers
Ranked customers by total_revenue with NTILE(3) to assign low, mid, and high spend tiers.
Summarised customer_count, tier_revenue, revenue_share, and total_orders by tier.
Exported tier summary to:
– data_clean/customer_spend_tiers.csv
